Carlo Alessi

Postdoctoral Researcher in Robot Learning

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Robot Learning at the Italian Institute of Technology (IIT), where I am part of the Humanoid Sensing and Perception group led by Dr. Lorenzo Natale.

My research focuses on deep learning for robot perception and manipulation, with applications in manufacturing and healthcare. I investigate imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and sim-to-real transfer across different robotic platforms, including humanoid robots, dexterous prosthetic hands, and continuum soft robotic arms.

My research was funded by national and international projects: ergoCub (INAIL), FAIR (PNRR-MUR), and PROBOSCIS (Horizon 2020).
